Filter Trail is a self-hosted web app for managing your personal garage and workshop. It helps you track vehicles, tools, and parts along with purchase details, costs, maintenance, and service history. Designed for hobbyists and home mechanics, it supports workflows like retiring assets (sold, scrapped, traded) while keeping a complete history of everything you own.
